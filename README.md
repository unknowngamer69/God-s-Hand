# God's Hand - CAD

God's Hand is a humanitarian hexacopter drone. It is being designed to carry supplies to people during natural and man-made disasters.

This repository contains the CAD models of the drone's body, base plate, arms, arm parts, and landing stand. The electronics and flight controller are part of a separate project.

![Drone V0.8 Assembly](https://cdn.hackclub.com/01a15244-0c02-78b9-98f5-3248820c9f1d/screenshot_2026-10-19_091008.png)


## CAD Models

### Main Body

The main body has an upper part and a lower part. Screws hold them together, so the body can be opened when needed.

![Main Lower Body](https://cdn.hackclub.com/01a12e18-2d46-73ef-8b6e-2d4ba654fec5/screenshot_2026-10-12_084126.png)

![Main Upper Body](https://cdn.hackclub.com/01a12e2b-eddc-7fa2-8030-37340384c263/image.png)

### Base Plate

The base plate sits below the main body. It connects the body, six arms, and landing stand. It is a separate part so the drone is easier to make and assemble.

![Base Plate](https://cdn.hackclub.com/01a1507e-1ea9-72e8-b7bf-f4494fee42d7/image.png)

### Arms and Arm Parts

The six arms are made as separate parts. Their design includes places to fit the motors and parts that hold the arms in place. Carbon fibre rods are planned for the arms to keep them light and strong.

## Main Parts

- Main upper body
- Main lower body
- Base plate
- Six arms and arm connectors
- Motor mounting parts
- Landing stand and connectors
- Camera mount (in progress)
- Supply container (planned)

## Design Goals

- Keep the drone light and strong.
- Make the parts easy to fit together.
- Keep the arms separate from the main body.
- Use screws and connectors to join parts.
- Make the parts easier to print and replace.

## Progress

- Landing stand: complete

- Final drone assembly: in progress

## Project Scope

This repository is for the physical CAD design only. It does not contain the flight controller or electronics design.
